Common Questions

Who is NCA designed for?
expand_more expand_less

NCA is for fitness coaches, nutritionists, personal trainers, healthcare professionals, and anyone who wants to build a serious, science-backed coaching practice. Whether you're a complete beginner or an experienced professional, the program is structured to meet you where you are.

Is NCA a certificate program?
expand_more expand_less

NCA is a mastership program — not a simple certification. You don't just receive a certificate; you undergo a rigorous educational journey covering 5 pillars of science plus business mentorship. A certificate is available upon completion, but the real value is the knowledge and network you gain.

How does the mentorship work?
expand_more expand_less

You get direct access to all four mentors — Amandeep, Ashley, Rishabh, and Siddhant — through live group calls, a private community, and direct messaging. No faceless automated responses. Our mentors are real practitioners who are invested in your success.
