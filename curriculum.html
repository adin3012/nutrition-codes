Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before joining Nutrition Codes Academy.

1 Are the sessions live or pre recorded? expand_more
All sessions are conducted live on Zoom, and recordings will be provided for later viewing and revision.
2 How will communication happen? expand_more
Communication will primarily be through WhatsApp. There will be a community group for discussions, networking, and resolving queries, and an announcement group for important updates and reminders.
3 Will there be additional course materials? expand_more
Yes, you will receive revision notes for every lecture, special coaching templates, textbook recommendations to deepen your understanding, and additional resources to stay updated on fitness trends and practices.
4 Who is this program for? expand_more
This program is suitable for everyone, from complete beginners to experienced fitness coaches. Whether you're looking to start your fitness journey, improve your knowledge, or advance your career, this course is designed to help you grow both personally and professionally.
5 What will I learn? expand_more
This program covers everything you need to excel in the fitness industry, including nutrition, exercise, coaching, business, social media, and research. You'll master topics like metabolism, specialized diets, and effective nutrition planning for various needs, such as hypertrophy and injury recovery. You'll gain expertise in anatomy, biomechanics, and training program design, along with injury management. The coaching module focuses on client communication, psychology, and business growth strategies. Social media lessons will teach you how to create impactful content, edit videos, and engage with your audience effectively. Finally, you'll learn how to analyze and apply research to stay at the forefront of the industry.
6 What if I have doubts? expand_more
You can ask your questions during live sessions, in the community group, or during dedicated Q&A sessions. If needed, you can also schedule personal meetings with the faculty to resolve your doubts.
7 How much time will I need to grasp the topics? expand_more
The program is self paced, but dedicating three to six months with consistent effort should be enough to build a strong understanding, especially if you actively participate in lectures and discussions.
8 When are the sessions held? expand_more
Sessions are typically held on weekends at 2 PM. The timing may be adjusted based on student consensus. One lecture per week is planned to give students ample time to absorb and practice the material.
9 What language will the sessions be in? expand_more
The sessions will be conducted in both Hindi and English to ensure every student can easily follow and understand the content.
10 When can I join, and how long is the course? expand_more
You can join anytime, and you'll have lifetime access to all course materials, including past, current, and future content. We plan to keep the course running for as long as the students need to learn, grow, and excel as fitness professionals. The program is continually updated with new topics and lectures to ensure the learning process never stops.
11 How much time will it take to finish the curriculum? expand_more
The curriculum is designed to be completed over the course of one year, allowing you enough time to thoroughly understand, apply, and master each topic while balancing consistency and practical learning.
