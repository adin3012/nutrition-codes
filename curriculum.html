Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before joining Nutrition Codes Academy.

1 Are the sessions live or pre recorded? expand_more
All sessions are live on Zoom, with recordings provided for later viewing and revision.
2 Who is this program for? expand_more
Everyone, from complete beginners to experienced coaches. Whether you're starting out, deepening your knowledge, or advancing your career, it's designed to help you grow personally and professionally.
3 What will I learn? expand_more
Everything you need to excel as a coach across five areas: Nutrition (metabolism, specialized diets, and nutrition planning), Exercise (anatomy, biomechanics, and program design), Coaching (client psychology, communication, and business growth), Social Media (content creation, editing, and audience growth), and Research (reading and applying the latest science).
4 When are the sessions held? expand_more
Typically weekends at 2 PM, with timing adjustable by student consensus. One lecture per week gives you ample time to absorb and practice the material.
5 What language will the sessions be in? expand_more
In both Hindi and English, so every student can easily follow the content.
6 When can I join, and how long is the course? expand_more
Join anytime, with lifetime access to all past, current, and future materials. The curriculum is designed to be worked through over about a year, with new topics and lectures added over time.
