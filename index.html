What Is Nutrition Codes

A mentorship for coaches who want to know why.

Nutrition Codes Academy is a science-first mentorship programme for anyone who coaches, or wants to. We built it because the fitness industry is full of confident advice and short on people who can explain the reasoning behind it.

Around 100 sessions cover nutrition, exercise science, coaching psychology, and the business of running a practice, taught from mechanism upward, in Hindi and English, with mentors who stay reachable long after the lectures end.
